**Definition.**
Let $\mathcal{K}$ be a class of finite structures. We say that $\mathcal{K}$ has the *Ramsey property* if for all $A, B \in \mathcal K$ and any $k > 0$ there is a $C \in \mathcal K$ such that $C \longrightarrow (B)^A_k$.

**Examples.**
1. The class of all finite graphs has the Ramsey property.
